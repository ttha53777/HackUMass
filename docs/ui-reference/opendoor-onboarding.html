How it works

First

You tell us what's going on, in your own words.

Tap what fits or type a sentence. We ask one thing at a time, and you can skip anything you'd rather not answer.

Then

We look at what's actually near you.

Your ZIP code narrows a hand-checked list of food, heat, housing and health programs to the ones that serve your town.

Finally

You leave with a plan you can start today.

What to do first, who to call, what to say, and which papers to bring. Every program links to its source.

Whatever it is, start there.

Pick the one that's loudest right now. You can add the rest once we get going.

A few promises, in plain words.

  • No account, nothing to remember.

    No sign-up, no password. Close the page and your answers go with it.

  • We never ask for your Social Security number.

    A ZIP code and a few rough answers are enough to point you the right way.

  • Straight about what's possible.

    We'll tell you a program might fit. Only the program itself can say yes, so we'll tell you how to ask.

  • Every listing is checked by a person.

    Programs come from a list our team checked by hand, each with a link to its source. Anything found on the open web is marked unchecked.

The door's open whenever you're ready.

About three minutes. You can skip anything, go back, or change an answer at any point.
