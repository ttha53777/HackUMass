## ma-good-neighbor-energy-fund
Source: https://www.magoodneighbor.org/assistance.html
- "available to any Massachusetts resident who, because of temporary financial difficulty, cannot meet a month's energy expense"
- "is not eligible for state or federal energy assistance"
- "Income must fall between 60 and 80 percent of the state's median income levels."
- "Income eligibility guidelines for 2026-2027 are as follows:"; table labelled "Total Gross Yearly Income"
- e.g. size 1: "$53,586 - $71,447"; size 4: "$103,050 - $137,399"; size 17: "$170,032 - $226,708"
- Apply: "contacting your local Salvation Army Corps Community Center" ; 413 area code line "800-262-1320"
- Western MA corps listed: Holyoke "(413) 532-6312", Springfield "(413) 733-1518", Greenfield "(413) 773-3154", Pittsfield "(413) 442-0624"
Source: https://www.magoodneighbor.org/faq.html
- "Those who qualify may receive up to $400 per household per heating season."
- "Requests for assistance go through The Salvation Army"
- "disburses available funds until the end of the summer or as long as there are funds available"
Source: https://www.magoodneighbor.org/
- "The Fund is currently open for the 2026-2027 season to all eligible households."
